## Feedback

Bug reports, reproduction steps, and references to historical sources are welcome. Share only information you have the right to disclose. Do not paste third-party code, scans, purchased assets, private agreements, or substantial copyrighted excerpts into issues without appropriate permission.

## Code and creative contributions

Arrange written contribution terms with Notation Systems Inc. through the repository maintainer **before submitting code, artwork, audio, narrative, or other copyrightable material for inclusion**. The agreement must identify the contributor, relevant work, applicable ownership or licence rights, permitted distribution, and any restrictions. No unsigned contributor agreement or automatic copyright assignment is created by this file.

Maintainers must not merge external material until the necessary permissions and provenance are recorded. Preserve upstream notices and flag any employer, client, co-author, or other third-party rights. Resolve ambiguity before accepting the work; do not replace another person's attribution with the project copyright holder.

Submitting a pull request does not, by itself, transfer copyright to Notation Systems Inc. or promise that the submission will be accepted. Participation does not grant a general licence to reuse the proprietary game or its assets. Rights arising under law, existing licences, and GitHub's applicable terms remain unaffected.

## Separately licensed components

A component deliberately released under MPL-2.0 or Apache-2.0 must carry its own exact scope, licence, notices, and contribution instructions. Follow those terms for that component rather than treating its licence as a licence for the entire game. No such component is designated by this licensing change.

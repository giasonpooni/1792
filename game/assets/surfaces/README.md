# CC0 surface study assets

The PNGs in this directory retain their upstream CC0-1.0 public-domain dedication.
They are NOT proprietary Cartesian Graphics artwork and are NOT historical evidence
for Gujranwala or any Punjabi location.

- Dirt: Charlotte Baglioni, https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt
- Plastered Wall: Amal Kumar, https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall
- Published asset licence: https://polyhaven.com/license
- CC0 deed and legal code: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

`sources.json` records upstream URLs and hashes, authors, conversion and output
hashes. The source 4096-pixel JPEG maps were resized to 1024-pixel PNG maps. Diffuse
maps are RGB; displacement and roughness maps are grayscale. This is a generic
material-appearance study, not authenticated regional fabric or acquired terrain.

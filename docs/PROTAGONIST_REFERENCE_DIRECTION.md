## Adult target

Use the mature design as the destination of the protagonist's visual development, not as a costume for every age. Preserve a recognizable open face, substantial layered headwrap, natural full beard, practical layered clothing, weathering and restrained ornament. The proposed palette is warm saffron/ochre against ivory, with dark worn leather and subdued metal. These colour names are artistic interpretation, not pigment analysis or a period-material finding.

The face must carry history without turning scars into decoration or hiding them behind cloth. Anatomical left and right must be recorded on the rig, not guessed from the image's screen sides. Actual visual impairment belongs to the existing character/vision contract; appearance in a picture is not proof of eye function.

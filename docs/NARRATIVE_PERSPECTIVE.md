# Narrative perspective: Buddh inside the public Ranjit

## The central experience

The player is Buddh Singh to himself. Public names, titles and claims belong to other people's relationships with him. The established naming resolver and stable character identity remain the implementation; this document adds the psychological direction, not another name or save system.

The campaign's pressure comes from **acting with incomplete access to a politically dangerous world**. A helpful patron can demand dependence. A relative can defend an estate against the player's appointment. A trusted reader can be competent yet selective. No surname, religion or gender automatically determines guilt, friendship or loyalty. The six selected women remain antagonist NPCs, not newly playable protagonists.

The player should distinguish “someone harmed me,” “someone benefits,” and “I know who ordered it.” These are different propositions. The world can establish a conspiracy without the player being told it, but the current childhood prototype establishes only an attack by an anonymous assailant. A full antagonist planning system is future work.

## Oral intelligence, not low intelligence

Lack of formal literacy is represented as a mode of access: a document is an object until somebody reads it aloud, describes it, or translates it. Memory, conversation, landscape knowledge, numeracy, seal recognition, command and political reasoning must not be reduced to an intelligence penalty. Recognizing a seal need not mean reading a letter; literacy itself can be script/language-specific when the writing system becomes richer.

Current implementation: an acquired message, a reading, a courier's limited testimony, source/channel/tick in remembered accounts, and no automatic independent corroboration from repeated speech. Readable subtitles and journal text are accessibility/presentation, not a contradiction of character illiteracy. Spoken audio is not implemented yet.

Later mechanics can let the player commission a second reading, seek the person who supplied a claim, compare a remembered instruction with what actually occurred, dictate a reply, and decide who may hear it. No lie meter or omniscient “conspirator” badge is implied. Psychological isolation should be produced through dependence, missing access and costly decisions, not by making every NPC secretly treacherous or arbitrarily falsifying controls.

## Faith as lived grounding

The requested portrayal is Sikh faith lived within Punjabi social and devotional life, not a universal orthodoxy/fervor meter. It can be present in remembered prayer, listening, companionship, restraint, gifts and recovery after danger. Shared spaces and cross-community relationships do not erase particular beliefs or turn all traditions into interchangeable decoration.

The present courtyard reflection is one original inner line, without scripture attribution, a combat buff or hidden-knowledge reward. Music, recitation, contextual devotional practices and historical locations need further authoring and review. Later ritual text/recordings should retain language, translation, performer and source provenance; generated dialogue is never labeled as historical Gurbani.

The Akali/Nihang institution remains a separately authored religious-military actor in the larger design. That does not make other groups devoid of religion, or make every encounter with that institution hostile. The clan/house/estate/command framework remains the default political structure.

## Limited sight and player experience

The selected Latif passage describes loss of an eye after very early smallpox, not a measured progressive decline during the tutorial. The current profile starts with that established limitation; no invented disease schedule is silently passed off as history. A later flashback/onset treatment or changing subjective burden can be authored explicitly, but is not implemented here.

Current presentation: mild asymmetrical left-edge framing with a clear-mode toggle (F4), unrestricted legible HUD/subtitles and normal controllability. No black half-screen, forced camera sway, fake total loss of depth on a 2-D display, random aim failure or blindness-as-insanity mechanic. The side/strength of the frame is an artistic convention, not a measured visual-field reconstruction. The gameplay inspection ray begins at character eye height rather than at the orbit camera; it does not implement a validated monocular field model.

Moorfields' patient information distinguishes monocular sight from losing half the visual field and discusses depth-cue adaptation. That supports rejecting a literal half-screen blackout, not certifying this shader as a medical simulation. Consultation: https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/for-patients/information-hub/advice-for-adapting-to-single-eye-vision

Future iteration should emphasize environmental reference points, voluntary head turns, spatial audio and readable attack telegraphs, with separate accessibility settings. Those aids should not reveal information unavailable to the character. Multi-perspective screenshots, contrast, comfort and disabled-player playtesting are needed before adopting a default experience.

## Tutorial as biography

The authored opening is practical learning before court mastery: find bearings, handle a message without reading it, ride, guard, counter, follow a trail, then return into danger. The same inputs used in practice must remain usable in the encounter. A small physical world becomes familiar before it becomes threatening.

The existing 1792 date is a scenario label, not a claim that each exercise and Latif's hunting-return ambush happened on that date. The new sequence is source-informed fiction. The real campaign can later connect this opening to households, command and Lahore without abandoning embodied play.

# Gujranwala construction and daily-use detail

This pass extends the merged courtyard, microdetail and material layers with small details visible during close inspection. It uses original procedural geometry and the existing workshop kit. The architecture, walking space, market operation and water-round operation retain their existing owners.

The eight closed courtyard doors receive paired dark ring pulls. Three have small latches; five existing thresholds receive uneven wear patches. The wear fits the imported threshold top rather than introducing a new step into the walking surface. The imported courtyard meshes, positions and materials are retained.

Two small open baskets occupy clear parts of the existing solid market counter. Their horizontal bands, flaring uprights, rim and base are actual geometry. Their holes remain open. They grant no goods and do not cover the inherited interaction position. The basket geometry is batched into one mesh per basket.

Three batches add masonry-course edges, a coping rim, a timber sheave and axle fittings to the existing well. All vertices fit its retained radial footprint and support height. Its cylinder appearance uses a separate mesh fitted inward by 25 mm so the course edges remain visible inside the original collision radius; disabling the pass restores the exact original mesh resource. The existing rope, water, bucket, drawing operation and ledger are unchanged. The sheave is a static construction study, with no independent animation or simulation.

F7 and the inherited greybox comparison remove and restore the entire layer. The layer has no timer or processing callback. Native qualification reports 18 mesh submissions and 17,166 triangles, within its 24-mesh and 20,000-triangle limits. That is a source budget, not a hardware performance measurement.

These are authored appearance studies. Existing research supports the broader household/courtyard setting; it does not authenticate these particular fittings, wear patterns, basket forms or lifting arrangements in Gujranwala in 1792. The [descriptive inventory](../data/art/gujranwala_daily_detail.v1.json) retains that boundary. No new historical claim or surveyed placement is admitted.

The native audit checks fitted geometry, actual collider support below every basket footprint corner, upward-facing threshold winding, retained source meshes, collision identity, clear inherited routes, F7 and greybox cycles, and unchanged campaign state and journal. The existing water-round traversal and carried-water pixel check remain in qualification.

The renderer captures three baseline/detail pairs at identical camera, lighting, tick and frozen campaign state. Only the new layer changes. It records PNG byte and decoded-pixel hashes, execution metadata and source commit/tree identities. The independent checker uses the existing strict PNG decoder and refuses broken PNGs, substituted images, changed camera/state metadata, incorrect layer labels or pairs with fewer than 100 changed pixels. Those checks establish capture integrity and consistency of the renderer's reported metadata; they do not independently reconstruct campaign state, authenticate history, or provide human playtesting.


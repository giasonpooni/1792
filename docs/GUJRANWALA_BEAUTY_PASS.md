# Gujranwala beauty pass v1

This increment develops the **aesthetic surface of the already-playable Home territory**.
It does not add another game loop, city simulation or historical claim.

## Visual goals

The existing authored courtyard was structurally readable but still visually repetitive.
This pass increases beauty through **contrast and composition**, not through more gameplay.

It adds:

- shallow ochre/plaster accent bands over the existing solid veranda wall;
- eight jali-depth studies with dark timber lattice and restrained medallions;
- six planted garden pockets breaking the long masonry rhythm;
- small flower/leaf masses for color and foreground depth;
- clustered vessels and folded/hanging textiles at the existing market counter;
- one low reflective basin as a visual cooling/washing cue, not a water simulation;
- four warm practical lights under the veranda, active only in golden-hour/evening F7 presets.

The intended palette is warm lime/plaster, dark timber, earth, muted saffron/rust,
blue-green cloth, and localized vegetation rather than uniformly saturated color.

## Composition rule

The pass uses the reference corpus as **visual grammar**, not direct reconstruction:

```text
pale wall mass
  + dark jali depth
  + localized textile color
  + planted green interruption
  + warm low practical light
  = a more layered, inhabited frame
```

Repeated bays remain legible, but no longer read as one undifferentiated wall.

## Reversible F7 ownership

The beauty pass lives under the existing authored-courtyard refinement.

- greybox comparison still removes it;
- authored/earlier toggles remain reversible;
- daylight keeps practical lights off;
- golden hour uses low warm practicals;
- evening strengthens them;
- cloth/foliage sample the existing chapter tick;
- no new timer or shader TIME is introduced.

## Physical/state boundary

The pass creates **no CollisionShape3D, StaticBody3D, NavigationRegion, inventory,
economy, knowledge, receipt, clock or save state**.

The existing courtyard pier collision remains authoritative.
All beauty geometry is presentation-only and deliberately placed at existing walls,
counters or edge pockets.

The low basin is visual only; it does not create water gameplay or alter the existing
household well/water-round authority.

## Historical boundary

The visual reference corpus supports ideas such as arcade depth, planted court rhythm,
decorative layering, pottery/textile clustering, jali screens and warm interior light.

This pass does **not** claim that the exact motifs, plant species, colors, lighting or
arrangement reproduce measured Gujranwala in 1792. Period-nearer evidence is still
required before final historical art approval.

## Remaining art work

This pass deliberately leaves major quality work open:

- production vegetation and species research;
- high-fidelity plaster/brick/timber modeling;
- sculpted pottery and textile assets;
- richer weathering maps;
- facial/hair/hand art;
- physically authored cloth;
- researched signage/markings;
- stronger distant skyline and horizon composition;
- human art review at gameplay camera distance.

The purpose of v1 is to move the scene from **structurally authored** toward
**visually composed and beautiful** without weakening its verified gameplay substrate.

# A road and a claim — physical caravan assignment

After the existing childhood, household inquiry, allowance and first food delivery,
the market offers **Escort via the disputed crossing**. The original direct return
option remains. Both use the **same single available carrier and supply contract**,
not additional cargo, troops, payment, treasury or simulation clock.

The carrier physically approaches a closed bar and stops. Stay within nine metres,
dismount, face the nearby roadkeeper and press **E**. A blocked sightline or looking
away prevents receiving his account. His statement is recorded as a claim; hearing
it twice is not a second witness.

| Choice | Physical and informational result |
| --- | --- |
| Recognize the local claim | The bar opens; the carrier takes the direct road. Local recognition is recorded, not ownership or annexation. |
| Ask for confirmation | The carrier waits. After 600 unpaused ticks (ten seconds at 60 Hz), return to the roadkeeper and hear the answer. Timer expiry alone gives no new knowledge or permission. |
| Take the field bypass | The bar remains closed, the same carrier follows the longer route, and the claim remains unresolved. |

Accompany the carrier home and check in at the original quartermaster. Original
storage-capacity requirements and one-time payment/supply settlement apply. There
is no new toll or optional-assignment bonus. Upkeep continues on the same clock,
so time and route choice can matter near a supply-watch boundary. The confirmation
delay is an authored test quantity, not a historical courier-speed model; no
physical messenger agent is implemented. The roadkeeper, claim, paths and dialogue
are fictional, not allegations about a named historical household or religion.
There is no armed checkpoint combat in this slice.

## State and executor

`road_state.gd` extends the unchanged Gujranwala authority through optional
`road_dispute` state (`caravan-road-dispute.v1`). It binds acceptance and completion
to the original economic receipts. Ordered waypoint arrivals retain index, tick
and position. Arrival is generated by actual bounded carrier motion, not entering
a UI result. Its source/decision/reply records generate Buddh's journal entries.
Narration remains a separate read-only presentation consumer.

The parent scene's original carrier movement is extracted into `_step_merchant`.
Its default behavior is unchanged; the road scene overrides that one executor for
the selected route. Navigation is invalidated when the physical bar opens/closes,
not every frame. The carrier cannot move past the unresolved checkpoint or be
checked in without completing its route.

## Saves and verification

F5/F9 use **`user://1792-shah-road-v1.json`**. J/F1 explicitly imports the previous
Gujranwala supply save without overwriting that slot or inventing an assignment.
Older saves keep the original direct route. The existing checkpoint sidecar
continues to replace the entire later world, discarding future money and knowledge.

Validation checks record shape, numeric JSON normalization, exact actor/contract
identity, tick order, route prefix, stopped-carrier position, and agreement with
original economic receipts. Invalid calls/loads leave the live world unchanged.
Spatial staging validates the **candidate's gate state**, restoring the live
collision mask before returning. A valid open-gate save is not rejected by the
currently closed bar, and a closed-gate save may not place an actor through it.
This is consistency checking, not signed anti-cheat or proof of the authenticity
of arbitrary externally supplied historical poses.

`test_shah_road.gd` covers three real UI/input/physics journeys, one per choice,
including mid-route save/load and final single check-in. They start from explicitly
labelled already-delivered-market fixtures; no pose/progress is injected after
departure. The inherited suites retain earlier tutorial and supply-route coverage.
Separate fixtures cover bar collision, facing/occlusion, opposite current/saved
gate states, malformed records, old-slot import and narration isolation. Five
render fixtures are retained separately from the played-route tests.

The scenario remains finite. Full combat, persistent generalized claim diplomacy,
unbounded economy, voice/audio, multi-cell terrain, gamepad support, human
playtesting and cross-platform performance qualification remain outside it.

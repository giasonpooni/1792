states that image binaries are not embedded and redistribution was not performed.

tiers and allowed-influence fields describe evidence use, not a copyright grant
or permission to reproduce source expression.

Reference images, video, scans, source prose, recordings, modern translations
and editions remain outside the source-ownership claim. Original project
annotations and qualifying original selection/arrangement may have separate
rights; facts, dates, names, ideas and methods themselves are not claimed.

# A short walk, a remembered lesson

This increment develops **HOME-013** and **HOME-014** in *1792: The Lotus Throne*.
It builds on the household story pass and retains the earlier opening and
childhood work. These are two existing objective arcs, not new mission counts.

The bazaar already has authored approach conversations, fight poses, sound,
listening gestures and an outcome-dependent walk Home. This pass extends that
direction. The horsecraft lesson already has real moving support, separate
weapons, reload conditions and a protected return to Home. Its new direction
gives those actions a clearer dramatic progression.

## HOME-013: what the friends remember

**Question:** What part of a quarrel will the three friends choose to carry Home?

The invitation establishes an ordinary short walk. The challenger turns that
promise into a public test. Mela's readiness and Jiva's caution remain distinct
without making either friend disloyal. The existing stand, withdraw and walk-away
outcomes retain their rules and canonical report.

After regroup, the player can stop beside the physical friends for a new
optional conversation, or hear it from the quartermaster's conversation while
both are present. Each outcome gets its own exchange. Mela gravitates toward
the impressive version; Jiva asks what their agreement to return together
actually meant. Buddh's reply brings the full account back into view.

This is a callback to the invitation, not a new moral score or a required
confession. The player can continue directly to the existing report. Conversation
pauses the world, leaves the camera under the established controls and adds no
combat result, reward or remembered testimony. It cannot speak through a missing
or obstructed friend.

The return is also easier to read physically. Guidance names the friend who
needs to be recovered instead of continuing to demand travel toward Home. Near
the actual conversation or regroup point it asks the player to wait for the
companion to close the remaining gap. A stationary nearby friend can make a
small wait gesture at the regroup approach when the other is still behind and
in clear sight. Neither actor is moved to make the composition work.

The existing bazaar director remains the single presentation channel. Moving
reduces explanatory copy; stopping restores it. A faced, visible strike warning
or counter opening takes precedence over incidental subtitles. A waiting line
starts only when that immediate cue clears and still expires if it becomes
irrelevant. Rejected local conversation actions get a brief visible recovery
message in that same channel. The bazaar also owns the foreground before the
household allowance and through its final acknowledgment, so another task
panel cannot sit beneath it. There is no new hidden deadline.

**Review route:** after the inquiry, meet the friends at the market. Accept the
walk, hear the challenge and either leave together or stand. A started fight
can also be abandoned by regrouping. Walk to the household approach with both
friends. Stop for the optional exchange, then return to the quartermaster.
Compare the three outcomes and try arriving ahead of one companion.

## HOME-014: confidence becomes attention

**Question:** Can a spectacular feat end with the same care that began it?

The three exercises now have distinct original teaching motifs:

| Exercise | Dramatic movement | Current demand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One horse | Confidence becomes listening to a living rhythm. | Establish movement and support, rise, then earn the standing hold. |
| Two horses | Balance becomes responsibility for more than oneself. | Attend to both horses and earn supported paired standing. |
| Mounted matchlocks | Display becomes concentration and a deliberate finish. | Fire each required slot standing, manage the active reload condition, then sit and stop safely. |

Brief remembered-Maha lines acknowledge actual milestones. The repeated idea of
attending to the horse changes meaning across exercises; praise cannot replace
the player's required hold, shots, recharges or withdrawal.

The task panel reads the real current stance, support and weapon state. It names
the next demand instead of presenting every instruction set at once. Unsupported
or recovering stance, an empty selected slot, a seated shot and a paused reload
receive different guidance. Movement keeps immediate controls visible and
reduces secondary explanation. Finite replies use the existing lesson clock.

Completing and returning produces a trainer acknowledgment of the learned
bundle. Leaving early and ending learned practice have different return lines.
The original progression remains: Home admits its single three-skill receipt
only after the complete lesson. There are no partial campaign capability grants,
extra horses or weapon inventory awards.

**Review route:** pass the first Home riding gate, stop and dismount, and speak
to the stable trainer. Complete the single-horse hold, continue to paired
balance, then complete all four standing shots and individual reloads. Sit and
stop before returning. Also leave an unfinished lesson and revisit learned
practice to compare the return acknowledgments.

The remembered feat remains an attributed tale with an unresolved exact
historical passage/date. The new words are original writing, not recovered
dialogue. Its source record and larger-than-life premise remain available;
this pass does not remove the tale because a citation is missing. The later
fixed father retrospective remains a separate sequence.

## Interaction and qualification

Bazaar answers are scoped to the dialogue that offered them. Queued choices
recheck local standing contact; a stale callback or remote friend cannot finish
an exchange or report. Original encounter receipts, outcomes, retry state and
character identities remain unchanged.

Horsecraft presentation adds no new clock, control executor, support rule or save
field. The retained-Home session continues to suspend the original world and
accept only the existing completed receipt. Standalone practice still cannot
grant Home capabilities.

Focused checks and inherited physical routes qualify behavior; native captures
qualify the inspected layouts and procedural gestures. Exact results are recorded
in the draft PR. The three new headless suites are registered in the normal
runner; native guidance and story renders are registered in the existing CI
workflows. Small-screen tests use real 800×450 viewports. Explicit fixtures
are identified separately from played routes, and a fixed inspection camera is
not described as a new player-camera journey.

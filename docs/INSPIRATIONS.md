# References / inspirational aspects

These are the project's selected creative muses, not affiliations, licensed assets, claims of feature parity or a mandate to reproduce complete games. The entries record what **1792 wants to learn from them**; they are design interpretations, not exhaustive accounts of those series.

| Muse | Aspect selected for 1792 | Development boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time | Readable movement, timing, physical spaces as training, personal narration. | No time powers; climbing and traversal still need their own implementation. |
| Red Dead Redemption series | Horse, home, travel, observed detail, relationships and an embodied protagonist. | Build one meaningful journey before a vast map. |
| Saints Row 2 | Familiar local territory becoming contested and visibly changing after player action. | Clans, houses and misls are not modern gangs; no instant permanent annexation. |
| The Elder Scrolls series | Discoverable places, local stories and exploration beyond the critical path. | Not a requirement for magic, a universal chosen-one structure or an encyclopedia of unreadable quests. |
| Grand Theft Auto series | Reactive open-world encounters, spatial memory, pursuit and freedom of approach. | No modern police/wanted system copied into eighteenth-century institutions. |
| Victoria 2 | Population, revenue, production and institutions as consequential long-run systems. | A later campaign layer, not an economic spreadsheet before the first ride works. |
| Assassin's Creed series | Physical access, climbing, infiltration and history experienced at ground level. | No supernatural information overlay by default; authored observations must have an access path. |
| The Witcher series | Characterful encounters, conflicting testimony and consequences beyond immediate victory. | No omniscient moral classifier or automatic transfer of its fantasy setting. |
| Far Cry series | Terrain, outposts and multiple encounter approaches. | A checkpoint must have specific people and claims, not merely respawning enemies around a capture flag. |
| Tom Clancy's Splinter Cell series | Deliberate observation, concealment, positioning and readable detection. | Light/noise/awareness mechanics need testing; a captioned quiet approach is not yet a full stealth simulation. |
| Shogun / Total War (retained earlier reference) | Formation command, campaigns, supply and military scale. | Strategic authority grows around the embodied game; no second unbound world state. |

The current childhood slice implements only walking/looking, a message mediated by speakers, riding practice, two guard/counter exercises, trail examination, quiet quarry observation and a bounded ambush/escape. The Lahore prototypes retain house conflict, command and companions. This list extends the prior direction rather than deleting it.

All characters, dialogue, geometry, code and content in the prototype are original or explicitly retained from 1792's earlier work. No assets, audio, levels or proprietary code from these games are imported by this document.

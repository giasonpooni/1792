# Historical Method

1792 is a historical game, not a documentary reconstruction. Evidence and invention must remain inspectable.

## Evidence classes

- **A — directly documented:** strong primary or reputable secondary support.
- **B — reconstructed:** inferred from established context but not documented at required granularity.
- **C — gameplay abstraction:** deliberate simplification for playability.
- **D — fictional connective material:** invented people, dialogue, incidents or spatial details connecting documented material.

## Record rule

Important historical records should carry source, date/range, evidence class, uncertainty note and affected game objects. Do not silently convert reconstruction into fact.

## Temporal integrity

Survival today is not proof of presence in 1792. Every landmark needs a construction/date check. Later Gujranwala interventions stay excluded from the start-year scene unless evidence supports an earlier precursor.

## Identity and chronology

The canonical player ID is `ranjit_singh`. Where source chronology conflicts—currently including Mahan Singh's death year—the conflict stays explicit in research records until reconciled. Game scripting must not erase the disagreement for narrative convenience.

# Historical Method

1792 is a historical game, not a documentary reconstruction.

The project should make the distinction between evidence and invention inspectable.

## Evidence classes

### A — directly documented

Supported by strong primary or reputable secondary historical sources.

### B — reconstructed

Not directly documented at the required granularity, but inferred from established historical context.

### C — gameplay abstraction

A deliberate simplification introduced for playability.

### D — fictional connective material

Invented characters, dialogue, incidents, or spatial details used to connect documented events.

## Rule

Important historical records added to the game should eventually include:

- source;
- date or date range;
- evidence class;
- uncertainty note;
- affected game objects.

Do not silently convert an uncertain reconstruction into canonical historical fact.

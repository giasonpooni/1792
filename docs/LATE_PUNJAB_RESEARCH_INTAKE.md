## Scope

The supplied compilation covers military resistance, outlaw traditions, community
labels, landscape and colonial policing in approximately 1848-1880. The table
separates supplied leads and integration questions from this limited source check.
It does not replace the compilation with a claim that every detail is verified.

Ranjit's childhood through the Lahore prelude remains first; his full life comes
next; DLC production follows. The anthology still closes in 1873. Later or undated
material stays in research. Religious figures are not embodied; religious sites
remain exterior-only; Sada Kaur and Adina Beg remain non-playable.

## Design invariants

Keep person, act, place, event date, report date, source, accuser, disputed variants,
jurisdiction and consequences separate. Legal classification must not directly
change karma, prove conduct or establish an inherited criminal trait. Conversely,
opposition to an authority does not automatically erase harm to another person.

Kinship, community, military employment, band membership and political allegiance
are distinct relations. Model named participants rather than hostile communities.
Extend the existing conduct and social-memory systems, not a new morality engine.

Original connective-fiction seed: a returning veteran renegotiates work, household
obligations and old military friendships. This is not a documented episode or a
claim about most veterans. Any later use must retain its fiction label.

Research buildings' changing uses, garments, markets, record rooms, routes and
vegetation by place and year. No terrain cell is marked loaded; no contemporary
landscape is relabelled historical; no childhood art is replaced by late material.
The geographic envelope and evidence-bounded 1:1 target remain protected.

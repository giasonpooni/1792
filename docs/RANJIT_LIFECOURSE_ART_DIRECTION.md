# Ranjit Singh — life-course visual direction

This document records a direct art-direction decision for the protagonist. It is not a
costume-history proof and does not replace period-nearer research.

The user-supplied adult Ranjit Singh reference is tracked in
`data/art/ranjit_reference_direction.json`. The binary image is not committed.

## Core visual thesis

**Power grows faster than personal spectacle.**

As Ranjit's authority expands, Lahore, furniture, guards, textiles, weapons,
diplomatic gifts and the people around him may become dramatically richer. Ranjit
himself should remain comparatively legible and restrained.

The player should be able to recognize the same person across decades because of:

- head and facial silhouette,
- habitual headwear logic,
- posture,
- weapon carry,
- gait,
- eye/face direction,
- preferred garment layering,
- relative restraint compared with his court.

Do not make historical importance synonymous with maximum jewelry.

## Life-course silhouette

### Buddh — child

- practical cloth and movement-first silhouette;
- little or no ornamental chest display;
- age-appropriate headwear and proportions;
- ordinary wear and repair visible;
- no adult facial hair;
- no miniature-court costume;
- training objects remain obviously training objects.

The child should belong to the same material world as the repaired baskets, faded
cloth and household work around him.

### Adolescent horseman

- slightly more deliberate waist/sash organization;
- better cared-for tack and weapon fittings;
- clearer field silhouette;
- still fundamentally practical;
- ornament, if present, remains small and specific.

### Young misl leader

- recognizable sword carry;
- higher-quality cloth rather than indiscriminate decoration;
- improved horse equipment;
- a small number of repeatable personal visual habits;
- campaign wear should remain visible after success.

### Lahore ruler

The **room** changes more strongly than the man.

Increase:

- carpet density,
- document furniture,
- guards and retainers,
- better weapon racks,
- textiles,
- diplomatic objects,
- seating hierarchy,
- court lighting,
- attendants' dress.

Ranjit may gain better cloth and selected jewelry, but should not automatically become
the most ornamented body in the frame.

### Mature Maharaja

Use the adult reference as a silhouette/mood anchor:

- light layered garment family;
- compact wrapped headwear;
- dark facial hair;
- visible sword/scabbard;
- composed standing bearing;
- restrained personal jewelry relative to later princely portraiture.

Exact fabric, color, date, ornament and cut remain research questions.

### Late life

Preserve identity through familiar objects and habits:

- headwear logic,
- face,
- sword/cane/support object where historically appropriate,
- habitual hand placement,
- posture changes,
- attendants adjusting around him.

Do not communicate age only by desaturating hair or adding a generic limp. Physical
change should affect staging, pace and the way others accommodate him.

## Court composition

Ranjit should often read as the gravitational center rather than the brightest object.

A useful composition is:

```text
rich textile / architecture / gifts
               ↓
ornamented courtiers and envoys
               ↓
        restrained Ranjit
               ↓
     everyone else's orientation
```

His centrality can be communicated through eye-lines, access, proximity, seating,
silence and who waits for whom.

## Material continuity

Objects should remember use.

High-value direct-craft examples that should survive later industrialization:

- a sword grip polished where the same hand closes repeatedly;
- a scabbard throat repaired with metal of a slightly different tone;
- tack patched twice with different leather;
- a sash faded where armor or a belt covers part of it;
- a chair arm darkened from habitual touch;
- a document weight made from an older damaged object;
- a carpet rotated rather than replaced after one edge wears;
- a jewel mounted in a newer setting;
- a court gift left unopened because its political meaning is uncertain;
- a cup retained for someone absent;
- a garment re-cut rather than discarded.

These should not become random procedural decals. Their value comes from **placement,
continuity and implication**.

## Temporal caution

The broader reference set includes later photographic and princely material. Use it for:

- silhouette families,
- jewelry placement,
- portrait posture,
- furniture vocabulary,
- relative textile density,
- weapon carry,
- court composition.

Do not silently copy later details into 1792 as exact evidence. Earlier game periods need
their own source qualification.

## Production boundary

Industrial workflows may later generate:

- clothing variants,
- wear maps,
- furniture families,
- court-layout candidates,
- jewelry combinations,
- LOD/collision.

Direct art direction retains authority over:

- which exception matters,
- how much ornament Ranjit wears,
- where wear tells a story,
- when asymmetry is meaningful,
- which object is allowed to remain unexplained,
- final silhouette,
- performance and staging.

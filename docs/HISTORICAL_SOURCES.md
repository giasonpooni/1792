## Latif is the selected biased account

The requested Internet Archive item `in.ernet.dli.2015.109845` is **Syad Muhammad Latif, History of the Panjab (1891)**. The user's later wording selects a **biased** account, not an unbiased authority. It is now the named source lens for the childhood prototype.

The ambush prototype uses an **unnamed fictional assailant**. Latif supplies the narrative precursor, not every world fact: lessons, witnesses, speech, shortened geography, three-hit attempt and escape are authored. The selected source's killing and annexation aftermath are not simulated. Whether the player learns an assailant's identity is separate from an author or historian naming him.

The Buddh/Ranjit personal-public naming convention remains the existing authorial policy. Latif's reviewed birth paragraph itself uses the name Ranjit; this book is not cited as proof of the game's delayed naming convention. The source record and game decision are retained separately.

## Authoring rule

Keep **source account**, **editorial interpretation**, **game-canon decision**, **world event** and **character knowledge** distinct. Bias is inspectable context, not a magical score that decides truth. A selected dramatic interpretation can be definite in a scenario while its historical basis remains attributed. Source prose and stereotypes are not automatically the voice of the game.

The source catalogue is not a mass importer. No third-party book PDF, restricted archive, private user conversation or copyrighted modern text is bundled into the public game by this change.

# The promise after the arrival

This pass develops **HOME-017: A Funded Instructor**, an existing sequence in
the childhood ledger. It brings the retained commission prototype into the
current Home chain, following the bazaar, remembered horsecraft, borrowed-rope
and remount work. It adds no mission to the inventory.

The dramatic question is **whether the household can keep the teacher it
wants, after it has persuaded him to come**. The decisive turn follows the
signing. A name in the household account still needs a place, a pupil,
provisions and actual attendance before it can become teaching.

The local instructor, agent, contract, receiving place and dialogue are
original fiction. Authored game coins are not historical wages. Neither the
commission nor a completed practice establishes a childhood incident or a
historical training doctrine.

## A person crosses the distance

The first scene is a promise made at the quartermaster. Reservation commits
existing household money; it cannot borrow an expected harvest or spend the
child's separate purse without an explicit contribution. Its immediate
purpose is legible: carry this commission to the agent. Detailed allocation
belongs in the optional accounts view.
The shared household menu offers a question about keeping an instructor.
That question opens a focused conversation with available coffers, the two
offers and a way back. Asking commits no money and takes no priority from an
already accepted responsibility.

The agent's introduction opens the next scene. It does not hire the candidate
on his behalf. The candidate is nearby, but accepting his terms remains a
separate situated action. The strongest voice is practical rather than
ceremonial: he wants the teaching place to be ready for whoever must stand
there. This gives the player something to remember after the price has left
the screen.

The walk home makes consent physical. The instructor is another moving body
in the current world. He follows through actual visual contact and collision
navigation; a fast departure can leave him waiting. An escort cue should name
that immediate separation and invite a return to him. Repeating the entire
commission explanation would bury the useful action.

The signing is a quiet threshold between a guest arriving and a teacher owed
service. Both people must actually reach the quartermaster. The contract's
wage reserve and recurring obligation remain real, while the scene's emphasis
is the promise Buddh has now made to a person in front of him.

## A lesson needs a place

The introduction's practical concern returns at practice. The player must
provide a guard pupil and the required materials, then keep Buddh and the
instructor together for **180 eligible ticks (three seconds at 60 Hz)**. The
guard is a static procedural projection of the reserved guard slot; he has no
independent pupil movement or combat performance. Attendance checks use the
actual Buddh and instructor bodies. This bounded practice demonstrates
funding and attendance; it is not a full combat tutorial or a mastery award.

An interruption changes the meaning of the earlier promise. A lesson that
stops because people are separated should ask for people to regroup. A
shortage before practice should identify the required provision. Unpaid or
unfed service should expose that current condition in household guidance;
it should not pretend the instructor remotely witnessed every ledger event.
In the limited instructor view, expenditure repairs require a local return
to Buddh. Buying food replenishes the stock; an unfed teacher or pupil must
still receive provisions at the next ordinary supplied watch. The task
distinguishes that wait from a missing purchase.

The repair is the payoff. On the transition from a genuinely stopped practice
back to eligible work, acknowledge the repaired condition once. Then let
the drill continue. The player's action supplies the resolution; a repeated
caption or an automatically repaired budget would weaken it.

The finishing line should leave the future open. One practice was supported
and performed. It is neither the last lesson this pupil needs nor proof that
the whole household can now maintain a trained force. The ordinary obligation
of another watch gives this small sequence its connection to a ruler's later
responsibilities.

## Attention, staging and literary direction

The recurring image is **the place kept ready**. At introduction it is a
condition of coming; at signing it is a household duty; at interruption it is
what has gone missing; at resumption it becomes something the player has
restored. The language should change with the action rather than repeat a
slogan word for word.

Each scene answers one immediate question and opens the next:

| Scene | What changes | What the player needs now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quartermaster promise | Available funds become a reserved commission. | Whether the chosen offer is affordable and where to present it. |
| Agent introduction | A person becomes available to meet. | Where the candidate is waiting. |
| Candidate consent | Proposed work becomes an agreed journey. | His practical concern and the need to accompany him. |
| Escort | Words become a return made together. | Whether he is still following and how to recover contact. |
| Signing | The household accepts an ongoing obligation. | The present pupil or material requirement. |
| Practice | Paid presence becomes performed teaching. | The current attendance or supply condition. |
| Interruption and repair | A promise is tested and made concrete. | One actionable cause, then one acknowledgement of resumption. |
| Completion | One supported practice enters the account. | The distinction between practice performed and mastery earned. |

The player retains movement and camera control. Conversation is an invited
pause. Secondary explanation should recede during travel, and the current
actor's task should remain readable in the limited instructor viewpoint.
An optional accounts screen may show the contract's complete allocation
without making the agent, candidate and quartermaster all recite it.

The desired visual rhythm is open lane, waiting candidate, slow shared walk,
quiet signing, then the ordinary space of work. Prototype positions and
procedural bodies can support this sequence now. Finished face and hand
performances, a distinctive signing gesture, expressive pupil drill,
recorded dialogue and final sound are further production work. No camera cut
or completed animation should be claimed solely from a text or state change.

Original dialogue may use domestic detail and short clauses. The instructor
can care about the pupil watching for a meal instead of watching his hand;
the quartermaster can ask whether the space is ready tomorrow as well as
today. These are writing directions, not recovered quotations. Do not place
technical authority, receipt or schema explanations in their mouths.

## Review the whole playable route

Launch `game/project.godot` in Godot 4.5.1 and choose **Home territory**. Finish
the household inquiry and accept the limited allowance. Settle any carried
load, active outing, workshop custody, riding session or other incompatible
responsibility before beginning the commission.

1. At the quartermaster, inspect the available commission offers. Compare
   the funded standard offer with an unaffordable senior offer from the
   initial allowance; first choose **Ask what it takes to keep an instructor**.
   A refusal must explain the shortfall without creating
   money. The existing food delivery is one route to earning more funds.
2. Reserve the chosen commission. Walk to the market agent and present it.
   Leave the conversation, approach the nearby candidate and hear his terms.
3. Accept the journey. Walk home beside the instructor. Let a corner or
   deliberate separation stop his following, then return to him. Finish the
   journey together and sign at the quartermaster.
4. Arrange a provisioned guard pupil and the required food and tool supply.
   Open the available drill conversation and inspect the current missing
   condition before repairing it.
5. Begin practice. Pause to confirm that waiting in conversation does not
   advance it. Deliberately break attendance, read the immediate cue, then
   regroup and see work resume. The resumed practice must retain its earned
   progress and consume no second starting fee or material allocation.
6. Compare the principal and limited instructor viewpoints at the available
   local transition. Both bodies stay in the same world, and the instructor
   receives his own participation record rather than Buddh's private journal.
7. Save and load during the commission and interrupted practice. Confirm
   the contract, bodies, current actor and earlier Home progress coexist.
   Return to Buddh through the local conversation and inspect the completed
   practice and ongoing household obligations.

Keep a separate review for cancellation, reserve release, accrued wages and
dismissal. Those options are practical exits with costs, not alternate happy
endings: paid agent work stays paid, dismissal leaves earned wages owed, and
releasing a reserve does not make future service free.

## Protected scope

- Finish Ranjit's childhood through the Lahore prelude first, then his complete
  life. Historical-character DLC production starts only after that narrative.
- Establish the full geographic envelope from the beginning. The target remains
  real metre scale and evidence-bounded, site-specific reconstruction; do not
  compress geography to conceal unfinished terrain or settlement work.
- The existing acquisition atlas is not loaded terrain. The retained compressed
  Home greybox is not a surveyed 1:1 reconstruction of Gujranwala.
- Religious figures are not embodied. Religious sites are exterior/view-only;
  prayer's bounded luck advantage depends on reviewed, period-specific site
  importance. Sada Kaur and Adina Beg remain non-playable.
- Visual beauty, movement, narrative and performance are co-equal production
  requirements. Beauty is not deferred to a final cosmetic pass.

See [historical-world implementation](HISTORICAL_WORLD.md) and the
[deferred anthology outline](ANTHOLOGY_PLAN.md).

## Visual identity and first benchmark

Proposed art direction: grounded, tactile, luminous historical realism. Establish
regional material and architectural identity rather than a universal sepia filter,
indiscriminate ornament or modern Punjab with historical costumes. This is an art
brief, not proof of any specific historical building or garment.

The current benchmark is one inhabited Gujranwala street, an adjoining household
courtyard and a stable. Establish three complementary views: intimate household
space; settlement edge/open country; bazaar approach. Source-specific footprints
remain research-dependent. Reusable construction kits do not establish historical
presence by themselves.

The benchmark needs a recognizable young protagonist and horse, convincing
silhouettes and garment/tack motion, coherent material scale, daylight and evening
lighting, grounded camera/movement and sound. Show ordinary playable views, not
only an offline render or an angle that conceals unfinished assets.

Build one small scene to the intended presentation standard, then build a second
using the same approved kit. Measure elapsed production effort, correction effort,
reuse and outside-player response. Neither test counts nor generated-file counts
measure accepted visual content throughput.

## Terminal-controlled production loop

NET coordinates work; Blender authors assets; Godot remains the active game,
rendering, simulation state and clock authority. C++, Rust, Python and Julia
providers remain bounded specialist integrations, not four replacement game loops.
Do not build a second renderer or repository-specific fork of the workbench.

Target workflow, not a claim of completed integration:

1. Select an evidence-linked scene, asset version and bounded parameter changes.
2. Rebuild only affected assets and export through the established game boundary.
3. Run a repeatable playable camera/movement route and named interaction.
4. Inspect images, clipping, animation, sound, collision and frame-time/memory data.
5. Accept or reject the revision; retain source, operation, execution and
   verification identities separately.

Garment and tack checks should include walking, running, crouching, mounting,
riding, fighting and sitting where the active controller supports them. The tool
must report unsupported checks rather than fabricate a successful capture.

A proposed reference-PC target is 1080p/60 fps; hardware, renderer and scene must
be recorded before qualification. Software-rendered CI screenshots do not prove
physical-GPU performance, console readiness or subjective artistic quality.

## Provisional release/content targets

These are editorial sizing targets, not completed content or time padded by travel.
The 1:1 map target and authored story length remain separate decisions.

| Release unit | Proposed authored play |
| --- | --- |
| Public visual/playable slice | 20-40 minutes |
| Substantial development build | 1-3 hours |
| Childhood to Lahore prelude | 8-15 hours plus exploration |
| Complete Ranjit narrative | 30-50 hours, subject to mission design |
| Short historical add-on | 2-4 hours |
| Substantial biography | 6-12 hours |
| Major regional/generational expansion | 12-20+ hours |

The planning conversation supplied the following uncalibrated, cumulative ranges
from the prototype stage. They are not an estimate derived from current accepted
content throughput. Sustained weekly capacity, art/animation support, licensed
assets and scope stability are assumptions; no calendar release date is promised.

| Milestone | Mostly solo + agents/selected contractors | Regular 3-5-person core + tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Convincing visual/playable slice | 4-8 months | 3-6 months |
| Substantial development build | 9-15 months | 6-10 months |
| Finished childhood to Lahore prelude | 24-42 months | 18-30 months |
| Strong complete-life game | 5-8+ years | 3-5 years |

Post-base-game DLC planning ranges depend on novelty, not release number:

| DLC workload | Mostly solo + tools | Small production core + tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short, same-region/period story | 4-8 months | 2-4 months |
| Substantial high-reuse biography | 9-18 months | 5-9 months |
| Major new region or mechanics | 18-30 months | 9-18 months |
| Large chronological/material-culture shift | 24-36+ months | 12-24 months |

Reuse should reduce repeated engineering, asset assembly, research and production
setup. It does not eliminate new writing, performance, regional/period art,
integration or playtesting. A six-to-twelve-month cadence for substantial
high-reuse DLC is a future hypothesis, not an announced commitment. Re-estimate
from the two-scene benchmark before using these ranges commercially.

## Patreon and public presentation

Patreon is a candidate funding channel once ordinary gameplay visibly demonstrates
the appeal of the world. No page, tiers, checkout, pricing, fee assumptions,
subscriber forecast or recurring deliverable has been launched by this plan.

Present Ranjit's game as the immediate product and the seven-century anthology as
the long-term direction. Potential benefits should reuse necessary production:
development films, art/material studies, research diaries, qualified preview
builds and credits. Do not promise monthly DLC or custom backer commissions.

The first additional production capacity should be chosen from demonstrated
bottlenecks, particularly environment/technical art or character animation, rather
than assuming more coding capacity alone produces finished presentation.

Sada Kaur and Adina Beg remain non-playable. Religious figures are not embodied. The Naurangabad and post-annexation stories use secular viewpoints only after identity/source review. The early Bhatti/Khokhar/Gakhar modules do not assert an unbroken descent merely from shared community names.

Baba Farid’s era frames the opening; the 1873 institutional epilogue frames the close. This does not claim “tribal Punjab ended” on that date. The 1857 Bar uprising remains a separate proposed chapter rather than being silently dropped. Central Asian captivity/exile and Singapore can be separately staged narrative locations; neither is advertised as part of a seamless surveyed mainland map.

Each chapter must distinguish observed evidence, attributed chronicle, oral tradition, disputed variants and original connective fiction. Lore is retained as lore, not excluded or falsely certified. Religious sites remain exterior-only. The original youth-story catalogue remains the binding childhood content inventory.

Architecture, clothing, language, kinship, trade routes, canals, river channels, land cover and allegiance must vary by place and time. A reusable mesh does not prove historical presence. Current catalogue entries are research scheduling records, not loaded scenes or DLC launchers.

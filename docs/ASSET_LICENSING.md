## Asset intake

Before integrating an external or commissioned asset, retain the following information in its provenance record:

| Field | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Asset identifier, repository path, and version or content digest. |
| Origin | Creator or supplier, source location, and acquisition date. |
| Rights | Actual rights holder, applicable licence or written permission, and the location of retained evidence. |
| Distribution | Commercial use, modification, redistribution, attribution, and other applicable conditions. |
| Review | Reviewer, decision date, and any unresolved restrictions. |

For commissioned work, retain the relevant agreement rather than assuming payment transfers copyright. For tool- or AI-assisted material, retain available creation records and applicable provider terms; do not infer exclusive ownership or originality from the tool used. Keep private agreements and personal information out of the public repository.

**Unknown rights status means not cleared for release.** Resolve it before bundling the asset. A separate asset licence or approved modding policy must explicitly identify any material intentionally shared with the community.

# Household gate passage — social meaning without a gate system

This direct-craft increment develops one existing childhood gate as a **staged passage** rather than inventing a general rank/queue simulation.

The physical gate already exists in the childhood route. The new layer adds presentation only:

- one household guard on the right edge;
- one waiting porter on the left edge;
- one handcart already tucked out of the passage;
- one wrapped pack explaining the porter’s pause without inventing a transaction;
- one modest cloth marker for material/wind motion.

## Childhood meaning

Buddh is not treated like a ruler.

On foot, the gate remains ordinary. When he approaches mounted, the guard makes a small hand signal and the porter yields a little farther outward. This is practical clearance, not deference, rank recognition or political authority.

Nothing is added to the campaign state.

## Why this is direct craft

A control plane can check that:
- the cart remains outside the lane;
- the porter never yields inward;
- no collision was introduced;
- the same scene remains state-neutral.

It should not decide:
- that the cart was already pulled aside;
- that the porter waits with one wrapped load;
- how large the guard’s signal should be;
- how much social meaning the childhood passage should carry.

Those are staging decisions.

## Life-course potential

The same architectural threshold can become biographically meaningful later.

A possible future progression is:

```text
childhood     → ordinary passage / practical clearance
young leader  → recognition / abbreviated checking
Maharaja      → route clears before arrival
```

Only the childhood case is implemented here. Later states require separate historical and narrative review.

## Historical boundary

This is an authored household-gate study in the compressed Home scene. It does not claim a measured 1792 Gujranwala gate, documented queue practice, or historical guard choreography.
